MINIMAL POLYNOMIAL AND WHICH ROOT IS MEANT
==========================================

    p(x) = x^4 - x^3 + 23 x^2 - 155 x + 158

Coefficients, lowest degree first, as they appear in the certificate file:

    [158, -155, 23, -1, 1]

theta is THE ROOT OF p IN THE OPEN INTERVAL (3, 4).

Why that names exactly one number
---------------------------------
p(3) = 81 - 27 + 207 - 465 + 158 = -46      (negative)
p(4) = 256 - 64 + 368 - 620 + 158 = +98     (positive)

so p has at least one root strictly between 3 and 4.

p'(x)  = 4x^3 - 3x^2 + 46x - 155,      p'(3) = 108 - 27 + 138 - 155 = 64 > 0
p''(x) = 12x^2 - 6x + 46,              discriminant 36 - 4*12*46 = -2172 < 0

p'' has no real root and positive leading coefficient, so p'' > 0 everywhere, so p' is
strictly increasing; since p'(3) > 0, p' > 0 on all of [3, 4]; so p is strictly
increasing on [3, 4] and the root there is unique.

p has two real roots in total, approximately 1.2571762 and 3.4678437, and two complex
ones.  The interval (3, 4) selects the second.  All four statements above are verified
exactly, in integer arithmetic, by verify.py before any certificate is examined.

Irreducibility
--------------
p is irreducible over the rationals, which is what makes Q[x]/(p) a field and the
inversions in the verification legitimate.  verify.py establishes it by exhausting the
two ways a monic integer quartic can factor: a rational root (rational root theorem,
every candidate tested) or a product of two integer quadratics (every integer
factorisation enumerated).

Consequences
------------
The Galois group of p is the full symmetric group on four letters, of order 24.  Q(theta)
therefore has no subfield other than Q itself, and the degree cannot be reduced: theta
admits no expression in a smaller field, and in particular none of the cyclotomic kind
that the seven-vertex value has.  This fact is not needed for the verification and is
stated only to explain why the answer looks the way it does.
