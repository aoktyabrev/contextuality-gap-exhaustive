MAXIMIZERS OF  Delta(G) = theta(G) - alpha(G)  OVER CONNECTED GRAPHS, n = 5 .. 10
=================================================================================

Each line is one graph, in graph6, together with its independence number, its exact
Lovasz theta, and the gap.  The eight-vertex row is the one this package certifies;
verify.py checks it and nothing else.  The other rows are given so that the eight-vertex
value can be seen in its place in the sequence.

Codes are in nauty's DEFAULT output labelling, which is the labelling of McKay's
published graph{n}c.g6 files.  graph6 encodes a LABELLING, not an isomorphism class:
a different but correct labelling of the same graph gives a different string, so compare
by isomorphism, not by string, unless both sides come from the same source.

MINIMAL POLYNOMIALS AND THE ROOT THAT IS MEANT

"deg" is the degree of theta over the rationals.  The interval isolates the intended
root; in every case the polynomial is irreducible over Q, so it is the minimal
polynomial and the degree cannot be lowered.

WHAT IS PROVED HERE AND WHAT IS ONLY REPORTED
---------------------------------------------
Proved inside this package, by verify.py, from exact certificates:

  n = 8   theta(GCQb`o) = the root of x^4 - x^3 + 23x^2 - 155x + 158 in (3, 4).

Reported, not re-proved here: the other five rows, and the fact that each row is the
maximum of Delta over ALL connected graphs on that many vertices.  Establishing
maximality requires enumerating the graphs, which is exactly what this package is
built to avoid; the enumeration and its certificates are in the repository named in
the accompanying letter.  Nothing in verify.py depends on any of it.

The eight-vertex graph, edge list (vertices 0..7), for anyone who would rather not
decode graph6 by hand:

  0-3  0-5  1-4  1-6  2-5  2-6  2-7  3-6  3-7  4-7

  alpha = 3; one maximum independent set is {0, 1, 2}.  The graph is 10 edges on 8
  vertices, degree sequence (2,2,2,2,3,3,3,3), |Aut| = 8, not vertex-transitive.
